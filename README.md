# Queen of Tears

## About the Series

**Queen of Tears** is my favorite *K-Drama series* because of its emotional storytelling, outstanding performances, and heartwarming romance. The drama follows the relationship between Baek Hyun-woo and Hong Hae-in as they overcome personal and family challenges while rediscovering their love for one another.

### Story Summary

**Queen of Tears** tells the story of a married couple facing struggles in their relationship. Through various challenges, they learn the value of understanding, sacrifice, and unconditional love. The drama combines romance, comedy, and family-centered storytelling, making it both entertaining and meaningful.
